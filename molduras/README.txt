Put your own frame (bezel) images here: .png or .jpg, ideally the size of
your monitor (for example 1920x1080 or 2560x1080).

In game, press F4 and use the FRAME option to cycle through them; after the
last image the frame is turned off. The "Bezel Artwork" mod must be enabled
in the launcher (Mods tab).

Images placed next to the executable or next to the image chosen in the
launcher are included in the list too.
